# Body Explorer privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Body Explorer does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No account.** You never sign in or create a profile.
- **No network access.** All anatomy models, names and licences are bundled inside the app. The app does not contact any server.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking.
- **On-device settings only.** Your preferences (for example the language or graphics quality) are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, export or delete on our side.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address before the change takes effect.
